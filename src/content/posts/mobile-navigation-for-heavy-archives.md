Mobile navigation should help readers move, not display the full information architecture in miniature. On a content-heavy site, the highest-value paths are usually search, latest posts, tags, authors, and the current article context.

A native-feeling menu is compact, labelled, and easy to dismiss. It does not need elaborate animation to feel good. It needs to preserve orientation.

## Make links obvious

The menu trigger needs an accessible name. The open panel needs a visible close path. Links need generous hit targets and enough contrast to work in a dark interface.

## Preserve the reading path

On article pages, mobile navigation should not fight the reading experience. A slim header, a progress indicator, and a short set of destinations are enough.
