The experience is content-first: a cinematic homepage, dense post cards, live local search, author pages, tag and category archives, article progress, sharing, and related recommendations.

Astroflix is designed to be a starting point. Replace this page, the demo posts and authors, and the public site configuration with your publication's own voice before launch.
